70+ Years of Refreshing Generations

Pakola made history on Pakistan's very first Independence anniversary celebrations in 1950 with its legendary green soda. For over seven decades, our distinctive flavours have brought families together across dinner tables, cricket victories, and festive weddings.

Today, our dairy innovations continue this legacy. We infuse that same celebrated pride into nutritious, grab-and-go flavoured milks that fit modern lifestyles while preserving the comforting flavours you grew up loving.

1950
The Birth of an Icon

Launched at Lawrence Road Karachi, instantly becoming the patriotic beverage of the country.

2006
Pioneering Flavoured Dairy

Introduced aseptic pure dairy cartons including the legendary Ice Cream Soda, Rose, and Strawberry.

Today
Exported to 20+ Countries

Sharing authentic Pakistani flavor nostalgia with communities globally across North America, UK, and the Middle East.
