## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the playful indulgence, heritage nostalgia, and creamy refreshment of flavored dairy beverages. Drawing direct inspiration from the iconic packaging, the aesthetic blends creamy pastel rose pinks with lush emerald foliage greens and dynamic crimson strawberry accents. 

The personality is upbeat, appetizing, wholesome, and premium FMCG:
- **Tone:** Joyful, refreshing, inviting, and deliciously tactile.
- **Audience:** Modern beverage enthusiasts, nostalgic brand fans, families, and young consumers seeking fun, flavorful, on-the-go refreshments.
- **Design Movement:** Modern Tactile Neo-Pastel. It pairs generous soft curves, creamy surface layers, and high-vitality fruit tones with clean, contemporary typography to keep the interface crisp, legible, and distinctly premium.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate taste association with strawberry dairy sweetness, grounded by brand heritage green.

- **Primary (`#D82036`)**: Vibrant crimson strawberry/rose. Used for high-impact call-to-actions, promotional splashes, highlight badges, and vital alerts.
- **Secondary (`#0D6E3F`)**: Signature heritage emerald green, extracted from the iconic logo identity. Used for trust markers, secondary CTA links, organic ingredient flags, and grounded brand accents.
- **Tertiary (`#F9C7D4`)**: Creamy pastel strawberry milk pink. Serves as tinted canvas backgrounds, container fill, feature card backdrops, and soft ambient pill fills.
- **Neutral (`#2B1E22`)**: Deep cocoa-berry plum black. Replaces harsh true blacks for body typography and high-contrast headlines, preserving the soft, organic warmth of dairy.
- **Milk White (`#FFFFFF`) & Cream Surface (`#FFF8FA`)**: The foundational canvas tiers that evoke smooth, whipped milk foam.

## Typography

The design system relies entirely on **Plus Jakarta Sans** for its friendly, organic geometries, wide apertures, and approachable warmth. It balances the playful consumer delight of flavored milk drinks with clear structural legibility.

- **Headlines:** Set in Bold (`700`) and ExtraBold (`800`) with tight letter-spacing to create punchy, high-impact billboard headers that replicate consumer beverage packaging punch.
- **Body:** Set with comfortable line heights (`1.5x` minimum) in deep berry neutral to maximize readability against creamy pastel backdrops.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Rendered with elevated weights (`600`–`700`) and slight positive tracking for crisp nutritional stats, ingredient pills, and pack volume meters.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid 12-column grid system governs desktop views, adapting to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Desktop: `>= 1024px` (12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2.5rem`, max content width `1280px`).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1023px` (8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`).
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`).
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows an 8pt rhythmic base. Component padding relies on generous horizontal breathing room (`space-lg`) paired with compact vertical offsets to keep product highlight panels bouncy and appetizing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft tonal layering rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows. The goal is to convey the physical, rounded weight of dairy cartons and splashes.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Light cream-white (`#FFF8FA`) background with subtle milk-pink gradient pools.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** Solid pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or tinted pastel pink (`#FCEEF2`), elevated with an extra-diffused, fruit-tinted shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(216, 32, 54, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Elements / Floating Badges):** `0 14px 32px -6px rgba(13, 110, 63, 0.12), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(216, 32, 54, 0.06)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Nutrition Popovers):** `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(43, 30, 34, 0.16)`.
- **Borders:** Subtle, warm milk outlines (`rgba(216, 32, 54, 0.08)`) separate cards without introducing clinical digital dividing lines.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-shaped (`3`) geometry philosophy, reflecting smooth dairy drops, flowing milk curves, and friendly consumer packaging.

- **Pill Contours (`rounded-full`):** Standard for buttons, flavor tags, category pills, nutritional claim chips, and pack size selectors (e.g., "235 ml").
- **Containers & Product Cards (`rounded-2xl` / `rounded-3xl`):** Heavy rounding (1.5rem to 2rem) creates soft, friendly silhouettes that accommodate bold splash imagery.
- **Visual Wave Accents:** Hero sections incorporate wave and milk-splash SVG masks to echo the liquid dynamic seen on packaging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Crimson strawberry (`#D82036`) background, crisp white typography, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#BF1A2E` with a micro-scale transition (`scale: 1.02`).
- **Secondary Button:** Deep emerald green (`#0D6E3F`) fill or stroke, white or emerald text. Ideal for retailer links ("Find in Stores") and brand story triggers.
- **Soft Accent Button:** Pastel pink fill (`#F9C7D4`) with crimson text (`#D82036`), used for playful low-priority interactions.

### Badges & Nutrition Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding.
- Employs tone-on-tone coloring: pale green background (`#E6F4ED`) with dark green text (`#0D6E3F`) for "100% Real Milk" or "Vitamin Enriched", and pale rose (`#FDECEF`) with crimson text for "Fresh Strawberry & Rose".

### Feature & Product Cards
- Smooth white or creamy pink backing, generous padding (`space-xl`), soft tinted strawberry shadow.
- Top section holds floating bottle/pack imagery breaking the upper card boundary; bottom houses high-contrast flavor titles, serving sizes, and quick "Buy Now / Store Locator" buttons.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Generously rounded (`rounded-full` or `rounded-xl`), bordered with faint pink-tinted grey, filled with pure milk white.
- Focused state transitions to a 2px emerald green glow (`#0D6E3F`), ensuring vibrant clarity and accessibility.

### Nutrition Highlights
- Compact circular or pill cards displaying calorie count, calcium percentages, and protein values with bold numbers (`Plus Jakarta Sans 800`) and muted neutral labels.
